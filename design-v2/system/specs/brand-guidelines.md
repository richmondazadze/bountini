# Bountini brand guidelines — 2.0

## The brand idea

Bountini turns a useful task into a clear invitation to contribute. “Small quests. Real rewards.” is the established tagline. The identity is precise, resourceful, confident and human. It should make a modest contribution feel worthwhile while keeping the operational facts visible.

Twofold is the master identifier: a compact stepped lowercase b. Its squared stem, 45-degree shoulders and open counter establish the visual vocabulary. The mark is an identifier rather than a picture of a coin, blockchain or AI service.

## Logo usage

Use the SVG masters in `assets/logos`. Horizontal is the default; wordmark-only suits contexts already using the symbol; stacked is for narrower layouts; symbol-only is for avatars, app icons and favicons. All lettering is outlined. The i tittles and stems have been squared and spacing optically adjusted from Switzer Semibold.

The symbol uses a 256-unit canvas. Stem width is 40 units. Clear space around the visible silhouette is at least 40 master units; horizontal lockup uses that same symbol stem unit. At logo height 64px, clear space unit is 10px. Avoid crowding the logo with a CTA, partner mark or edge.

Digital minimum: master symbol 24px; horizontal signature 160px wide; wordmark 120px wide; stacked 140px wide. Use pixel-aligned small symbol at 16–23px. Print minimum: symbol 6mm; signature 35mm wide. These are starting minima verified visually in rendered examples; fabrication may require larger minimums.

Approved: ink on white/mist, white on cobalt/ink, cobalt on white. Reversed symbol has an optical cut for larger display use. One-colour and black variants work in restricted production. Platform avatar/icon masks are controlled by the platform; supplied canvases are square.

Do not stretch, rotate, outline, add glow/shadow/gradient, change the counter, retype the wordmark, or embed a photo inside the logo. Use a clear solid region on photographs. Do not add ® without verified registration. This visual process does not constitute trademark clearance.

CMYK values are mathematical approximations, not press-ready conversions. Obtain a printer profile and proof cobalt; use K-only small black body text where appropriate. No Pantone match is asserted. Product surfaces typically use approximately 80% white, 15% neutral structure and 5% cobalt. Campaigns may reverse that balance with one bold cobalt field.

## Typography

Switzer 400/500/600/700 is the working family; Instrument Serif 400 is the expressive family. Product UI and all financial/timing data use Switzer. Short campaign/presentation headlines use Instrument Serif. Optional serif italic is limited to short emphasis. Two families maximum.

Product scale: 48/52 H1, 32/40 H2, 24/32 H3; body 16/24; lead 20/30; labels/captions 14/20. Marketing display: 96/96 desktop, 56/56 mobile. Use −0.025em tracking for large UI headings, natural tracking for serif and body. Never use display serif for dense instructions, fields or rewards.

Fontshare describes its fonts as free for personal and commercial use: https://www.fontshare.com/about . Obtain Switzer from https://www.fontshare.com/fonts/switzer and retain the official licence. Switzer binaries are not redistributed in this kit. The HTML uses the official hosted stylesheet with Arial fallback. Instrument Serif is supplied with its OFL licence from https://github.com/Instrument/instrument-serif . Review deployment and redistribution rights for each final channel. The outlined logo has no runtime font dependency.

## Layout, graphic devices and imagery

Square panels and media. Control radius 2px maximum. 8px rhythm with 4px optical spacing. Desktop 12 columns / 24px gutters / 64px margins; tablet 8 / 24 / 32; mobile 4 / 16 / 20. Reading width 640px, forms 720px, application 1280px, marketing max 1440px. Use whitespace before extra containers. Flat page surfaces; floating overlays may use a restrained shadow.

The step band and open window derive from Twofold geometry. Crop them at campaign scale; keep pattern density low and out of reading regions. Icons are 24px, 1.75px square-cap outlines. Use one cobalt emphasis with ink structure. Decorative icons are hidden from assistive technology.

Photography: real contributor process, hands/tools, natural light, neutral setting, purposeful crop. Obtain consent and rights. No generic crypto coins, fake proof screenshots or decorative finance spectacle. Informative imagery needs contextual alt text. No stock photography is bundled.

## Voice and motion

Speak with warmth and operational precision. Use “Create a quest”, “Review before funding”, “Approve & pay”, “Payment confirmed”. Describe why an action is unavailable and how to recover. Use “Bountini suggests” for AI assistance. Do not promise guaranteed income or automated fair decisions. Devnet rewards are test SOL, not cash value.

120ms feedback, 180ms disclosure, 240ms overlay, 600ms brand entrance. Ease cubic-bezier(.2,0,0,1). Small 8px movement, no bounce on money. Reduced motion removes transforms/stagger. No autoplay audio. The logo resolves to its exact master geometry.

## Cross-channel templates

Slides: 1920×1080 / 16:9, 96px margins, 12 columns, 32px gutters. Cover, divider, statement, two-column, demo, data, timeline, quote and closing layouts. Minimum body 28px. One message per slide. Use actual evidence; placeholder layouts must not become invented traction.

Social: 1080 square, 1080×1350 portrait, 1920×1080 landscape. OG: 1200×630. Key content 8% inside edges. Email signature uses horizontal master plus readable role/contact text. Stationery A4 / 20mm margins, logo approximately 40mm wide. Business card 85×55mm plus printer-specified bleed. Supplied examples are templates; replace placeholders before publishing.
